# Field Report Demo Images

Photorealistic starter examples for the four current Gemma4Good field report categories. These demo images avoid visible people, hands, faces, silhouettes, and crowds so they are safer for repeatable model demos.

## Files

- `water-quality-turbid-water.png` - water quality example with visibly turbid water.
- `sanitation-standing-wastewater.png` - sanitation example with standing wastewater and litter near a public water point.
- `infrastructure-leaking-pipe.png` - infrastructure example with a leaking pipe or valve.
- `health-risk-water-queue.png` - health risk example with unattended open containers, poor drainage, waste, and muddy standing water. The filename is retained for URL stability.
- `category-examples-contact-sheet.png` - original generated 2x2 source sheet.

## Generation Prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Gemma4Good field-report demo photo set, four separate-looking realistic examples in a clean 2x2 contact sheet for preview.
Primary request: Generate one realistic demo image for each field report category visible in the app: water quality, sanitation, infrastructure, and health risk. These must contain no people.
Scene/backdrop: Humanitarian water reporting context in Somalia or the Horn of Africa, natural daylight, phone-camera realism, documentary field-photo style.
Composition: 2x2 grid, each quadrant a different realistic mobile field photo. No stylized illustration, no UI, no labels, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Absolutely no people, no hands, no faces, no silhouettes, no crowds, no human figures.
Quadrant 1, water quality: close view of visibly turbid brown water filling a yellow plastic jerrycan or shallow basin at a public tap, realistic sediment and reflections, no person present.
Quadrant 2, sanitation: unattended public water point or drainage channel with standing wastewater, mud, and scattered litter nearby, realistic but not graphic, no people present.
Quadrant 3, infrastructure: damaged blue water pipe, leaking valve, or broken hand pump with wet ground and pooling water, practical field-report framing, no people present.
Quadrant 4, health risk: unsafe water collection area with stagnant muddy puddles, open containers, poor drainage, and scattered waste around an unattended water source, no people present.
Style: photorealistic, natural light, slightly imperfect smartphone camera framing, high detail, documentary, believable shadows, realistic materials.
Avoid: any people or human body parts, text, captions, icons, cartoon style, cinematic overprocessing, gore, injury, disease symptoms, branded products, exaggerated disaster imagery.
```
