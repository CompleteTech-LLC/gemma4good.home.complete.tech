# Field Report Demo Images

Photorealistic starter examples for the four current Gemma4Good field report categories.

## Files

- `water-quality-turbid-water.png` - water quality example with visibly turbid water.
- `sanitation-standing-wastewater.png` - sanitation example with standing wastewater and litter near a public water point.
- `infrastructure-leaking-pipe.png` - infrastructure example with a leaking pipe or valve.
- `health-risk-water-queue.png` - health risk example with a queue near poor drainage and muddy standing water.
- `category-examples-contact-sheet.png` - original generated 2x2 source sheet.

## Generation Prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Gemma4Good field-report demo photo set, four separate-looking realistic examples in a clean 2x2 contact sheet for preview.
Primary request: Generate one realistic demo image for each field report category visible in the app: water quality, sanitation, infrastructure, and health risk.
Scene/backdrop: Humanitarian water reporting context in Somalia or the Horn of Africa, natural daylight, phone-camera realism, documentary field-photo style.
Composition: 2x2 grid, each quadrant a different realistic mobile photo. No stylized illustration, no UI, no labels, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
Quadrant 1, water quality: close view of visibly turbid brown water in a plastic jerrycan or shallow container near a public tap, realistic sediment and reflections.
Quadrant 2, sanitation: public water point or drainage area with standing wastewater and litter nearby, realistic but not graphic.
Quadrant 3, infrastructure: damaged water pipe, leaking valve, or broken hand pump with wet ground, practical field-report framing.
Quadrant 4, health risk: people queuing near an unsafe water source with muddy puddles and poor drainage, realistic distance framing, no identifiable faces.
Style: photorealistic, natural light, slightly imperfect smartphone camera framing, high detail, documentary, believable shadows, realistic materials.
Avoid: text, captions, icons, cartoon style, cinematic overprocessing, gore, injury, disease symptoms, identifiable children, branded products, exaggerated disaster imagery.
```
